*A four-page data brief on the August 2026 employment situation and what it means for computational biology. Built from the BLS release of September 4, 2026, BioSpace's Q2 job-market report, CompBioJobs' Q2 postings data, and MassBio, CBRE and Lightcast trackers. Read the composition, not the totals.*

> **In one line** — Payrolls jumped +162,000, five times the 12-month pace; biopharma cuts fell 59% in Q2 while R&D payrolls kept growing; 631 computational-biology postings skew hard to senior; Massachusetts headcount fell 3.1% in 2025 and the 2026 posting rebound is measured against that trough. The worst is over. The rebound isn't here yet.
